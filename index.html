Without durable memory

Every new task starts from scratch.

  • re-read old threads and documents
  • ask the same “why?” again
  • risk missing a team rule or decision

With Vault

The next task starts with the relevant past.

  • find the decision when it matters
  • prepare a short, inspectable summary
  • mark the guidance useful
No made-up numbers

How teams can measure the value.

This page demonstrates the memory loop—not a promise about customer savings. It does not call an AI model or pretend to know what a dollar is worth. A real deployment can measure that separately.

In a real deployment, Perseus Ledger can record what a provider charged and the prompt Vault replaced. That makes the comparison reproducible. Smaller context without a bill is reported separately as an estimate, with the coverage and counting method shown.
